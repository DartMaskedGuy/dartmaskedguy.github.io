01 · About

Engineering reliable, beautiful mobile
experiences for the real world.

I'm a Mid-level Flutter Developer with over four years of experience designing and shipping cross-platform mobile applications for the Nigerian consumer market — from live, real-time game shows watched by tens of thousands of concurrent players, to courier and logistics platforms coordinating riders across cities in real time.

My work sits at the intersection of clean architecture and high-stakes reliability: WebSocket event streams that can't drop a beat, payment flows that have to be airtight, and HLS video pipelines that need to feel instant. I lead with BLoC state management, deliberate component decomposition, and a strong bias toward minimal dependencies.

Lately I've been pulling that same rigor into VR/AR experimentation — exploring where spatial computing and real-time mobile architecture meet, with an eye on founding something of my own in that space.
